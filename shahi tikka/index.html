Our Story

Taste the Royal Legacy

Shahi Tikka was born from a simple belief — that great food deserves great fire. Nestled at Gatwala Chowk on the Lahore–Faisalabad highway, we've become the must-stop destination for travellers and locals who know that no journey is complete without our tikka.

Every piece of meat is marinated for hours in our signature spice blend, then kissed by the open coal grill to achieve that perfect smoky char. No shortcuts. No compromises. Only the finest cuts and freshest ingredients.

The Shahi Experience

More Than a Meal

Open Late Every Night

We're open from 9 AM to midnight — whether you're starting your day or ending it, Shahi Tikka is ready with sizzling food fresh off the grill.

Family-Friendly Space

Our restaurant offers a welcoming, clean environment with dedicated family seating — the perfect setting for celebrations, gatherings, and everyday dining.

Dine-In & Takeaway

Enjoy your meal in our comfortable dining area, or grab a hot takeaway pack for the road. Perfect for highway travellers and nearby residents alike.

Master Grill Chefs

Our seasoned chefs have perfected the art of open-flame BBQ over years of practice — every dish leaving our kitchen is a testament to their craft.

Fresh Daily Preparation

No frozen shortcuts. We prepare all marinades, masalas, and curries fresh each day so every bite carries the full depth of flavour.

Spotless Hygiene

A clean kitchen is the foundation of great food. Our spotless facilities and food-safety standards ensure every meal is as safe as it is delicious.
