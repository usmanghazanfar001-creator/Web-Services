Our Story

A Journey
Across Asia

Sachi — meaning "bliss" in Japanese — was born from a simple belief: that the diverse cuisines of Asia, when brought together with care and creativity, can create something truly extraordinary.

Located in the heart of Faisalabad, our kitchen is guided by chefs who have trained across Japan, China, Thailand, and beyond — bringing authentic flavors home and reimagining them for the modern palate.

Every dish tells a story. Every meal is an invitation to travel without leaving your seat.
