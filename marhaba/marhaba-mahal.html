Our Story

From a Humble Lounge
to a Royal Legacy

The name "Marhaba" emerged from Babu Jee's small lounge — a place where warmth, flavour, and community were inseparable. What began as a modest dream gradually grew into a customer convenience chain that became Faisalabad's most iconic dining destination.

This house has witnessed everything that went around the city. With integrity and values woven into its very foundation, we serve every guest as our privilege — presenting traditional desi cuisines and BBQ, freshly prepared with organic ingredients and special in-house recipes that give food lovers an unforgettable experience.

With Marhaba Mahal, we revolutionize the dining concept — making every meal a whole Royal Experience, worthy of Lyallpur's rich heritage.

House Signatures

Dishes That Define Marhaba Mahal

Mutton Karahi

The crown jewel of Marhaba Mahal. Premium mutton cooked in a blazing karahi with tomatoes, green chillies, and our signature masala. Unmatched in Faisalabad.

Dumpukht

An ancient Mughal preparation — meat sealed in a vessel and slow-cooked in its own juices and aromatic spices until fall-off-the-bone tender.

Special Kebab

Marhaba's handcrafted minced meat kebabs — a closely-guarded recipe that has earned thousands of loyal fans over the years.

Fish Tikka

Freshwater fish fillet marinated in a vibrant tikka blend and grilled over charcoal to golden perfection — a must for seafood lovers.

Desi Murgh Karahi

Country chicken from the tandoor to the karahi — the freshness of the bird and our fresh tomato-chilli masala create something truly special.

Batair (Quail)

A rare delicacy on any menu — tender quail marinated in our spice blend and grilled until crispy outside, juicy within. A true Mahal exclusive.

Mango Lassi

Thick, creamy, and bursting with fresh mango — our legendary lassi is praised by guests as the best in Faisalabad. The perfect end to any meal.

Marhaba Karahi Tikka

Our signature creation — tikka marinated meat finished inside a fresh karahi masala. The best of both worlds, only at Marhaba Mahal.
